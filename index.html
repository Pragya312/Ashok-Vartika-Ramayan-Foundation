ABOUT US

Ashok Vatika Ramayana Foundation

Ashok Vatika Ramayana Foundation, based in Delhi (NCR) / Greater Noida, Bharat, is a global seva organization affiliated with Rashtriya Seva Samvardhan Samiti, Vishva Hindu Parishad (VHP), and driven by the principle of Seva Parmo Dharmah. Certified by Shri Ganga Raju Ji (President) and Shri Ajay Kumar Pareek Ji (Secretary), and guided by the motto "Seva Dharmah Param Gahanah" and "Mata Sita Se Sanskar, Ramayan Se Samadhan, Sewa Se Vishva Kalyan", we work for Dharma Prasar through the Digital Ashok Vatika Rath (VR) and Ramayana Pathshalas across 50 countries.

For Seva, we proposed the Jirnoddhar of the ancient Sita Temple, Sita Dharmashala (20–50 rooms), Sita Rasoi, and the 108 Ashok Trees Vatika at Sita Eliya, Sri Lanka. Through Sita Shakti, we empower 1 Lakh daughters, and promote Samrasta by reviving the Ramayana Corridor connecting Ayodhya (Bharat), Janakpur (Nepal), and Ashok Vatika (Sri Lanka) with an annual Bharat Delegate Yatra on Sita Navami.

Our Vision

To build a harmonious society rooted in the timeless values of the Ramayana—dharma, compassion, and service—empowering communities across India and globally through heritage, education, and sustainability.

Our Mission

To preserve and promote the cultural, spiritual, and ethical teachings of the Ramayana by fostering education, service, and community outreach, inspiring individuals to live with compassion, truth, and responsibility.

Know More
Target by 2030: 100 Ramayana Research Centres globally, Ramayana Path Gaman Cultural Tourism, 1 Lakh Daughters connected with Sita Shakti, and 1 Lakh Ashok Trees planted across Bharat & Sri Lanka.

Connecting 15M NRI youth with VHPA, HSS UK, establishing Sita Clubs in 20 universities, and UNESCO heritage circuit development.
